Welcome to the ASME AI Screener

This platform is an autonomous AI agent designed to evaluate deep-tech and hardware startups precisely how ASME Ventures does.

The AI scours the web to research a company and scores it on a 1-5 scale across 5 specific metrics extracted directly from ASME Ventures' public investment thesis:

  • Focus Area Alignment: Do they fit into Digital Solutions, Intelligent Automation, Hardware, Energy, or Safety Tech?
  • Built World Impact: Do they advance human progress in the physical world and drive industrial transformation?
  • Engineering Innovation: Do they possess deep operational intelligence and engineering credibility?
  • Early-Stage Fit: Are they an early-stage startup?
  • ASME Synergy: Can they benefit from ASME's global mechanical engineering network?

How to use it: Browse the pre-seeded companies, or click the + Add Venture button to run a live AI analysis on a company of your choice (e.g., Boston Metal or Form Energy). The AI takes about 20 seconds to crawl the web, extract signals, and generate a graded rationale.
