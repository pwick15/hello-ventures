Automating Venture Intelligence

Imagine a venture analyst that works 24/7 without taking a break.

Instead of paying an analyst to scour the internet 8 hours a day, this application drastically cuts research costs and boosts productivity by fully automating the candidate screening pipeline.

How it works:

  1. Input: Provide just the name and URL of a company.
  2. Research: An autonomous AI agent immediately scours the internet, reading their website, LinkedIn, and news articles to extract structured data.
  3. Analysis: The agent deeply analyzes that data and evaluates it against ASME Ventures' 5 core investment metrics:
    • Focus Area Alignment: Digital, Automation, Hardware, Energy, or Safety.
    • Built World Impact: Advancing physical world infrastructure.
    • Engineering Innovation: Deep operational and technical credibility.
    • Early-Stage Fit: Optimal startup stage viability.
    • ASME Synergy: Benefiting from our global mechanical engineering network.
  4. Grading: The AI grades the venture on a 1-5 scale and writes a customized rationale.

And just like that—you've completely analyzed a venture in under 20 seconds.
